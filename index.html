STREAMBEAUTY

Privacy Policy

Real-time beauty enhancement for Windows

Last updated: November 21, 2026

1. Overview

StreamBeauty is a Windows desktop application developed for personal use. It provides real-time camera enhancements and facial retouching using Snap Camera Kit and a custom Lens. This policy explains the application's handling of data.

2. Camera Access and Video Processing

StreamBeauty accesses a connected camera to display a live preview and apply real-time visual enhancements. The developer does not operate a backend service that collects or stores camera footage or recordings.

Camera Kit technology is provided by Snap. Its processing and handling of information are subject to the applicable Snap policies and technical implementation.

3. Personal Information

StreamBeauty does not require users to register an account or provide their name or email address to use the application. The developer does not maintain a database of personal profiles.

4. Local Storage

Application preferences, camera settings, beauty settings, and Camera Kit credentials may be stored locally on the Windows device. Where implemented, protected credential storage uses Electron and operating-system security functionality.

5. Third-Party Technology

StreamBeauty uses Snap Camera Kit for augmented reality and facial enhancement effects.

For information about Snap's privacy practices, visit Snap's Privacy Policy .

If camera output is transmitted using OBS Studio or another streaming platform, those third-party services may process, transmit, record, or store that content according to their respective policies.

6. Data Sharing

The developer does not sell personal information or operate a service that distributes users' camera recordings. Integrated third-party technologies may handle data as needed to provide their functionality.

7. Data Retention

StreamBeauty does not maintain developer-operated server storage for camera recordings or user profiles. Locally stored application data may remain on the device until it is cleared or the application data is removed.

8. Security

StreamBeauty is designed to operate locally and use available Windows security mechanisms where applicable. No software system can guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when application features, integrations, or data-handling practices change. The latest policy will be published on this page.
